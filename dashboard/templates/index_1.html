About this dataset

  • Market-verification categories retain their original definitions. They are not NLI support probabilities or guarantees of truth.
  • Unscored claims do not contribute to accuracy or verification-score denominators. Undefined rates display as unavailable.
  • Sentiment and NLI results are separate saved cohorts; their totals need not match the market-verification dataset.
  • Dates, when exported, use the existing POST_DATE field; a comment may inherit its parent post's timestamp.
  • The event calendar opens official sources; it does not imply that this dataset contains reactions to those events.
  • Open the results export.
